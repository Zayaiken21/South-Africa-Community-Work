# Community Work South Africa identity

The South African flag-inspired location pin combines local opportunity with the national flag’s red, blue, green, gold, white and black. The converging green paths suggest people coming together. This is a community platform identity, not a government emblem.

Use the supplied SVG lockups for crisp scaling. The existing outlined wordmark is preserved. Full-colour marks remain the same on light and dark backgrounds. Keep at least one quarter of the icon width clear around the logo. App and maskable icons have additional safe padding.

The header, footer, CV builder, favicon, social image, and app/store assets use this identity. The existing app navigation, job tools, and affiliate integrations are retained.
