**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

Find paid work close to home — or hire people by the shift.

Community Work is a South African job and shift marketplace. Workers pick up paid hourly shifts from local businesses in one tap, with no waiting to be chosen. Businesses post shifts, book reliable workers and pay safely through escrow.

FOR WORKERS
• Pick up paid shifts near you in one tap — restaurants, retail, warehouses, events, cleaning and more
• Clear pay per hour in rand, always at least the national minimum wage
• Pay for escrow shifts is secured before you start and paid to your bank after the shift
• A free-cancel window with a live countdown if your plans change
• Browse in-person jobs from South African employers and online jobs open to South Africans
• Save jobs, build a professional CV for free, and download it as PDF or Word
• Your own profile with photo, skills, experience, languages and availability

FOR BUSINESSES
• Post a shift in minutes: duties, place, date, hours and hourly rate
• Workers book themselves — no endless messages
• Pay into escrow when someone books; release pay after you confirm the hours
• Block workers who don’t meet your standards, and see each worker’s profile and CV
• One account for working and hiring — switch with a tap

FAIR AND SAFE
• Community rules based on South African labour law: 4–12 hour shifts, overtime and Sunday pay, meal breaks and safe workplaces
• Strikes for late cancellations and no-shows keep shifts reliable; anyone paused can appeal
• Nobody pays to work — report anyone who asks
• Help from Randy, our support assistant, or talk to a person

All nine provinces. Distances in miles from your area. Works on any phone, even with a weak connection.
